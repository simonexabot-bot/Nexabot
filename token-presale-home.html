IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

Three balances, three different meanings.

Virtual USDT

Simulation-only portfolio value used to illustrate arbitrage trades. It is not blockchain USDT.

Simulated profit

An illustrative result produced by paper trades. It is not guaranteed income or withdrawable real-world profit.

ANXB token

The planned ecosystem token for participation and promotional benefits, subject to formal launch terms.

ANXB FAQ

Understand before registering interest.

Does ANXB guarantee arbitrage profits?

No. ANXB does not guarantee returns and is not a claim on simulated or future trading profits.

Is registering interest a purchase?

No. It is non-binding and does not reserve tokens, collect money or authorize a wallet transaction.

Is the contract live?

No verified contract is presented on this page. A network and audited contract address must be formally published before launch.

Why are final tokenomics not shown?

Supply, allocation and vesting must be approved before exact figures are advertised.
