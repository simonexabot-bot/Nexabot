Token purchase and utility disclosures.

This operational draft requires qualified legal review for every permitted jurisdiction.

Purchase and eligibility

Presale participation requires account, identity, jurisdiction and sanctions eligibility. Registration or contact does not guarantee allocation. Payment must be independently verified before approval, and delivery is complete only after the required blockchain confirmations.

Geographic restrictions

ANXB must not be offered where token sales, digital assets or the intended utility are prohibited or require authorization that has not been obtained.

Refunds and cancellation

Contact requests may be cancelled before payment approval. After delivery, refund rights depend on applicable law and published sale terms. Blockchain transactions may be irreversible.

Utility limitations

ANXB is intended for approved ecosystem products. It does not represent equity, ownership, debt, guaranteed access, guaranteed liquidity, passive income or a right to arbitrage profits.

Risk and value

Token value can fall to zero. Technical, liquidity, regulatory, custody, smart-contract and counterparty risks may cause loss or prevent use.

Tax responsibility

Customers are responsible for obtaining independent tax advice and reporting purchases, rewards, transfers and disposals.

Privacy and retention

Purchase, eligibility, wallet, delivery and compliance records are retained only as required for operations, security and law.

Complaints

Use the documented support channel for delivery, eligibility, refund or security complaints. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

No guaranteed profit

ANXB ownership does not guarantee returns from the simulation, academy, arbitrage activity or any future product.
