Customer responsibility

Customers should understand their selected model, trading settings, fees, permissions, and risk limits, and should only use amounts they can afford to lose.

Wallet permissions can create security exposure. Read the exact transaction/approval request in your wallet, use permission limits where available, and revoke permissions you no longer need.
