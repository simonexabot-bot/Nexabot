ACADEMY FAQ

Before you enroll.

Are the courses financial advice?

No. They are educational programs and do not recommend personalized trades or promise results.

How do early members get free access?

Accounts created before the configured early-member cutoff receive complimentary course enrollment.

Do I need coding experience?

The foundations do not. The bot-builder program introduces architecture progressively and emphasizes paper testing.

Does completing a course activate the bot?

No. Education and the arbitrage portal are separate workflows.
