COMMON QUESTIONS

Learn with clear expectations.

Are these courses financial advice?

No. NEXA Academy provides general education and simulated exercises, not personalized financial, investment, legal or tax advice.

How much do courses cost?

Current individual courses range from $49 to $100, with equivalent internal ANXB product-credit pricing shown clearly. Eligible early members receive complimentary access.

Do I need trading or coding experience?

No. Trading Basics and AI Trading Basics are designed for beginners. Builder courses list their recommended prerequisites.

Does completing a course guarantee profit?

No. Course completion, certificates, simulations and ANXB ownership do not guarantee trading results, token value or income.

Is the arbitrage bot part of the academy?

It is a separate virtual simulation product in the broader ecosystem. The academy teaches concepts first and links to the existing simulation as an optional next step.
