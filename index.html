How it works

A visible path from account setup to ongoing control.

NexaTrade organizes requests, records and controls. Registration does not guarantee eligibility. Live custody or execution is available only where lawful and through separately approved providers.

Know the boundaryStrategy settings are instructions and risk limits—not promises of profit. Digital assets can lose some or all value, and configured stops may not prevent larger losses during gaps, outages or low liquidity.
Read the full risk disclosure →
  1. 01

    Create a secure account

    Register with your own details, verify your email and protect your password. Staff will never request a password, seed phrase or private key.

  2. 02

    Complete eligibility and identity review

    Provide required identity, jurisdiction, suitability and source-of-funds information. Access may be limited or declined to meet legal, compliance or provider requirements.

  3. 03

    Review disclosures, fees and provider terms

    Read the applicable risk disclosure, fee schedule, custody or wallet terms and consent records before requesting activation.

  4. 04

    Choose a permitted funding method

    Use provider-issued deposit instructions or prove control of a compatible wallet. Entering a transaction hash is only a review request and does not create account credit.

  5. 05

    Select a strategy and set limits

    Choose an available risk profile, supported assets and a maximum allocation. Examples and targets are illustrations, not forecasts or guaranteed outcomes.

  6. 06

    Start in paper mode where available

    Paper trading can demonstrate strategy behavior without live execution, but simulated fills may differ materially from real prices, fees, liquidity and slippage.

  7. 07

    Monitor activity and records

    Review ledger entries, positions, fees, request status and provider references. Valuations can be delayed or inaccurate when market data is unavailable.

  8. 08

    Pause, stop or request withdrawal

    Submit control and withdrawal requests from the portal. Processing remains subject to available balance, security checks, compliance review, network conditions and provider timelines.

Common questions

Know before you begin.

Does NexaTrade guarantee returns?

No. Digital assets and automated strategies can lose some or all invested capital. Historical or illustrative results do not predict future performance.

Does connecting a wallet give NexaTrade control?

No. The current wallet flow proves ownership through a signature that cannot transfer funds. Any future delegated trading permission must be separate, explicit, limited and revocable.

When does a deposit become part of my balance?

Only after the transaction is verified and approved. Entering a transaction hash is a request for review, not proof of payment.

Can I withdraw part of my balance?

Yes. Eligible customers can request full or partial withdrawals from available ledger balances. Security, compliance and provider processing may apply.

Can I stop the bot?

The portal supports pause, stop and emergency-stop requests. Effective live execution depends on the integrated provider and backend worker.

Is the service available everywhere?

No. Eligibility depends on jurisdiction, identity verification, suitability, applicable law and provider availability.
