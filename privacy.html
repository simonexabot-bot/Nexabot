Privacy Notice

Effective date: To be confirmed before launch. Replace this draft with a jurisdiction-specific notice reviewed by privacy counsel.

Information collected

The live service may collect account and contact details, identity-verification information supplied through a provider, device/session information, public wallet addresses, transaction records, support communications, and consent/audit records.

How information is used

Information may be used to operate and secure the service, meet legal/compliance obligations, prevent fraud, communicate with customers, and maintain accounting/audit records.

Sharing and retention

Information may be shared only with vetted service providers, identity/compliance providers, custody/trading partners, and authorities when legally required. Define retention periods, international transfers, customer rights, and contact routes before launch.

Security

No system is completely secure. Do not send recovery phrases or private keys to NexaTrade. Report a suspected security issue through the support channel published at launch.
